Scraped on: 2026-09-29
Official Body: All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)

===========================================================================
OVERVIEW
===========================================================================
The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) is the statutory body
that regulates technical education in India. As the apex regulatory body,
AICTE prescribes minimum qualifications, pay scales, service conditions, and
career advancement schemes for faculty in AICTE-approved technical institutions
(degree-level and diploma-level).
The eligibility norms have been updated through a series of Gazette Notifications
aligned with successive Pay Commission recommendations (5th CPC, 6th CPC, 7th CPC).

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS FOR APPOINTMENT (Degree-Level Institutions)
Based on AICTE Regulation 2019 - 7th CPC (Current / Most Recent)
===========================================================================

--- A. ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY DISCIPLINES ---

1. ASSISTANT PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- First Class (or equivalent grade) in Bachelor's degree AND
  First Class (or equivalent grade) in Master's degree in the
  relevant branch of Engineering / Technology.
- OR: First Class Master's degree in relevant discipline.
- Ph.D. is desirable but not mandatory for initial appointment.
- Candidates with Ph.D. in the relevant subject may be exempted from
  the requirement of First Class at Bachelor's or Master's level.
- Candidates without Ph.D. must clear National Eligibility Test (NET) or equivalent.
Pay Band: Level 10 (Rs. 57,700 - 1,82,400) as per 7th CPC

2. ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in the relevant discipline. MANDATORY.
- First Class (or equivalent) at Bachelor's and Master's degree levels
  in the relevant branch.
- Minimum 8 years of experience in teaching / research / industry,
  of which at least 2 years must be post-Ph.D.
- At least 2 research publications in SCI / SCIE / Scopus indexed journals.
Pay Band: Level 13A (Rs. 1,31,400 - 2,04,700) as per 7th CPC

3. PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in the relevant discipline. MANDATORY.
- First Class (or equivalent) at Bachelor's and Master's degree levels
  in the relevant branch.
- Minimum 10 years of experience in teaching / research / industry.
- At least 6 research publications in SCI / SCIE / Scopus indexed journals.
Pay Band: Level 14 (Rs. 1,44,200 - 2,18,200) as per 7th CPC

--- B. COMPUTER SCIENCE / INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY / MCA ---

1. ASSISTANT PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- First Class Master's degree in Computer Applications (MCA) or
  Computer Science / Information Technology, OR
- First Class (or equivalent) B.E./B.Tech. in Computer Engineering /
  Computer Science and Engineering / Information Technology AND
  First Class M.E./M.Tech. in relevant discipline.
- Ph.D. (MCA/Computer Science) is desirable.
NOTE: As per Gazette Notification XIII (07.11.2022), MCA holders with
Ph.D. are eligible for appointment at higher levels.

2. ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in Computer Science / IT / Computer Applications. MANDATORY.
- First Class at Bachelor's and Master's levels.
- Minimum 8 years teaching/research/industry experience.
- Minimum 2 publications in SCI/SCIE/Scopus indexed journals.

3. PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in relevant discipline. MANDATORY.
- Minimum 10 years of experience.
- At least 6 publications in SCI/SCIE/Scopus indexed journals.

--- C. MANAGEMENT / MBA ---

1. ASSISTANT PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- First Class (or equivalent) in Master's Degree in Business
  Administration or equivalent management qualification (MBA or
  equivalent), OR
- First Class CA / ICWA / CS with First Class MBA / equivalent.
- Ph.D. is desirable.

2. ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in Management / Business Administration or related discipline. MANDATORY.
- 8 years of experience in teaching / research / industry.
- At least 2 SCI/SCIE/Scopus publications.

3. PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in relevant discipline. MANDATORY.
- 10 years of total experience.
- At least 6 SCI/SCIE/Scopus publications.

--- D. PHARMACY ---

1. ASSISTANT PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- First Class (or equivalent) Master's degree in Pharmacy (M.Pharm)
  in the relevant specialization.

2. ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in Pharmacy (relevant specialization). MANDATORY.
- 8 years of teaching/research/industry experience.
- Minimum 2 publications in indexed journals.

3. PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in Pharmacy. MANDATORY.
- 10 years of experience.
- At least 6 publications in indexed journals.

--- E. ARCHITECTURE ---

1. ASSISTANT PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- First Class Bachelor's degree in Architecture (B.Arch).
- Registered with the Council of Architecture (COA).
- Master's degree in Architecture / Planning or equivalent is preferred.

2. ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in Architecture / Planning, OR
- Master's degree in Architecture + 8 years relevant experience.
- 2 publications / significant design works.

3. PROFESSOR
Minimum Qualifications:
- Ph.D. or extensive professional accomplishment.
- 10 years experience.
- Publications / significant contributions to the field.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS FOR APPOINTMENT (Diploma-Level Institutions)
Based on AICTE Regulation 2019 - 7th CPC
===========================================================================

1. LECTURER (equivalent to Assistant Professor at Diploma level)
- First Class Bachelor's Degree in Engineering / Technology in the
  relevant discipline, OR
- First Class Diploma in the relevant discipline with at least 2 years
  of industry / teaching experience.

2. SENIOR LECTURER / HEAD OF DEPARTMENT
- First Class Bachelor's Degree in Engineering / Technology.
- Minimum 5 years of teaching / industry experience.
- Master's degree in relevant discipline preferred.

3. PRINCIPAL
- First Class Bachelor's Degree in Engineering / Technology.
- Minimum 15 years of experience (including 5 years in administration).
- Master's degree required; Ph.D. preferred.

===========================================================================
CAREER ADVANCEMENT SCHEME (CAS)
Based on AICTE Gazette Notifications V, VI (2012), VII (2016), XI, XII (2020)
===========================================================================

ASSISTANT PROFESSOR (Stage 1 to Stage 2):
- 4 years of service as Assistant Professor at Stage 1.
- Satisfactory performance in Annual Performance Index (API) scoring.
- Minimum API score requirements as prescribed.

ASSISTANT PROFESSOR (Stage 2 to Stage 3):
- 3 additional years of service at Stage 2.
- Minimum API score.
- A published paper or significant contribution.

ASSISTANT PROFESSOR (Stage 3) to ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR:
- Completion of Ph.D. (if not already completed). MANDATORY.
- Minimum 3 years at Stage 3.
- API score requirements met.
- Interview / selection process by duly constituted committee.

ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR to PROFESSOR (CAS):
- 3 years of service as Associate Professor.
- Research publications as per prescribed norms.
- Interview by selection committee.

MAJOR / CORE BRANCHES OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
Based on AICTE Gazette Notification VIII (28.04.2017)
===========================================================================

AICTE has defined Major/Core Branches of Engineering & Technology for the
purpose of determining relevant/appropriate qualifications for teaching
appointments. Faculty must hold degree(s) in branches relevant or appropriate
to the subject they teach.

Recognized Major/Core Branches include:
- Civil Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Electronics & Communication Engineering
- Computer Science & Engineering
- Information Technology
- Chemical Engineering
- Metallurgical Engineering
- Mining Engineering
- Aerospace Engineering
- Biomedical Engineering
- Biotechnology
- Environmental Engineering
- Industrial Engineering

===========================================================================
NON-TEACHING STAFF QUALIFICATIONS
===========================================================================

LIBRARIAN:
- First Class (or equivalent) in Master's in Library and Information Science (MLIS).
- NET / SLET / SET is desirable.
- Minimum 2 years professional experience for Senior Librarian.

PHYSICAL EDUCATION DIRECTOR:
- First Class Master's degree in Physical Education (M.P.Ed.).
- NET / SLET / SET qualification desirable.
- National/International representation in sports is an advantage.

TRAINING & PLACEMENT OFFICER:
- First Class (or equivalent) MBA or equivalent management qualification.
- OR First Class Engineering degree with relevant experience.
- Experience in corporate relations and placement activities.

===========================================================================
KEY NOTES AND CLARIFICATIONS
===========================================================================

1. RELAXATION FOR INDUSTRY EXPERIENCE:
Relevant industry experience in a reputed organization may be counted
towards the teaching experience requirement at the discretion of the
appointing institution, as per AICTE norms.

2. FIRST CLASS DEFINITION:
First Class means 60% or above marks (or equivalent grade/CGPA) at
the graduate and postgraduate levels. For SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates,
relaxation in minimum marks may be applicable as per government norms.

3. Ph.D. REQUIREMENT:
Ph.D. is mandatory for Associate Professor and Professor positions.
For Assistant Professor positions, candidates without Ph.D. must clear
the National Eligibility Test (NET) or equivalent, wherever applicable.

4. NET / SLET EXEMPTION:
Candidates with Ph.D. awarded through a regular program as per UGC
norms (2009 regulations) are exempted from NET/SLET requirements for
Assistant Professor appointments.

5. MCA / Ph.D. AMENDMENT (2022):
As per Gazette Notification XIII dated 07.11.2022, the qualification
criteria for MCA holders with Ph.D. were amended to enable their
eligibility for higher-level positions (Associate Professor / Professor)
in Computer Science / IT / related disciplines.

6. INDUSTRY EXPERIENCE CREDIT:
Each year of work experience in a reputed industry / R&D / Government
organization at appropriate level is counted as equivalent to one year
of teaching experience for the purpose of direct recruitment.

7. PUBLICATIONS:
- Publications must be in journals indexed in SCI / SCIE / Scopus
  for engineering disciplines.
- For Management, publications in ABDC / ABS / UGC-listed journals
  are considered.
- Books published by reputed publishers and book chapters are
  considered separately from journal publications.

8. ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE INDICATOR (API):
The API scoring system evaluates faculty across three categories:
Category I - Teaching, Learning & Evaluation Activities
Category II - Co-curricular, Extracurricular & Professional Development
Category III - Research & Academic Contributions
